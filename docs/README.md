# Lex Consuasor Website

Professional Legal Consultation & Corporate Compliance Services

## Overview

This is the official website for Lex Consuasor, a leading legal consultation firm established in 2010. The website provides information about our services, team, and how to contact us.

## Website Structure

The website consists of the following pages:

- **index.html** - Home page with services overview and firm introduction
- **about.html** - Information about the firm, mission, values, and expertise
- **services.html** - Detailed description of all legal and consultation services
- **team.html** - Meet our experienced advocates and consultants
- **contact.html** - Contact information and inquiry form
- **privacy.html** - Privacy Policy and data protection information
- **terms.html** - Terms of Service and legal terms

## Services Offered

- Legal Documentation
- Corporate Business Consultation
- Corporate Compliance Services
- General Legal Services

## Styling

The website uses a professional, clean design with:
- Responsive layout that works on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices
- Professional color scheme (navy blue, gold accents)
- Easy navigation between pages
- Clear, readable typography

## Features

- Fully responsive design
- Professional navigation bar
- Contact form with client inquiries
- Team member profiles
- Comprehensive service descriptions
- Privacy and Terms pages
- Social media links (Facebook)

## Getting Started

To view the website, open `index.html` in a web browser. All pages are connected through the navigation menu.
